## Other findings today
- **TTA (test-time adaptation) ablation.** It helps both the choice (@.10) and the localisation (@.01):

  | variant | dev |
  |---|---|
  | selection only | +4.28 |
  | refinement only | +4.26 |
  | both | +4.71 |

  - About half of the gain is a global channel re-weighting. Weights averaged over unlabeled trn pairs give
    DV++-fast at ~8 ms/pair: test +3.81.
  - The learned weights down-weight SD s4/s5 and up-weight DINO.
- **TTA hurts the supervised DIY-SC features.**

  | | with TTA | without TTA |

  It helps all zero-shot features: CleanDIFT SPair-U +1.67 with vs +0.98 without; CleanDIFT AP-10K +3.73.
  This is reported as a limitation. No change was made, because DIY-SC is never used for selection.
