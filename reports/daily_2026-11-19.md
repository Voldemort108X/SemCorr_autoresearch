## What failed (and why) — details in journal/DAY1.md, analysis_4/8/13.md
- Mirror swaps (≈ 6.7 % of all keypoints, ~25 % of remaining errors) are untouched by every decoder: for dv's mirror
  errors the correct part is in the top-30 pool only 18 % of the time and all appearance cues prefer the wrong side; the
  2-D offset of a mirror pair is ≈ chance for side-view animals / bicycle (near vs far side).
- ~35 further variants within noise: MNN-affine / MLS / dense-flow / ICM-MRF geometry, one-to-one, mirror-pair swap,
  NMS-peak pools, query smoothing, SD/DINO reweighting, K, τ, quadratic / up-sampled refinement, relational profile,
  handedness direction, pool-adaptive penalties, arm selection from decoder evidence.
- T3 pitfall found and fixed: bigger models looked +1.2 better on dev but were memorising the dev images (shared with
  trn_train); an image-disjoint A/B protocol showed no gain → model selection now uses that protocol.
