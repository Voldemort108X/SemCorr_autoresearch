**DV++ (seq 17, training-free).**
1. Per-pair test-time adaptation (TTA), no labels. Learn diagonal channel weights for the pair's descriptors
   (20 Adam steps) to maximise the dual-softmax mutual-match confidence of the masked source and target.
2. Flip ensemble: a second view of every query. In the horizontally flipped source, the mirrored counterpart part
   looks like the query part. This view is mixed into the forward row (α .75) and the refinement map (.5).
3. Déjà Vu cycle (λ .5), plus the same cycle measured in the flipped arm (.25).
4. Pair-level consensus: a RANSAC similarity fitted over all query keypoints' candidate pools, scored as a truncated
   residual (μ .5, cap 5 patches).
5. Reverse-rank penalty (.02·log1p).
6. All keypoints are decoded in the pair's adaptive-flip arm.
7. Window soft-argmax refinement: window 2, β .03.

## What failed, and why (details in journal/DAY1.md, analysis_4/8/13.md)
- **Mirror swaps.** They are ≈ 7 % of all keypoints and ~25 % of the remaining errors, and no decoder or TTA touches
  them:
  - For Déjà Vu's mirror errors, the correct part is in the top-30 pool only 18 % of the time.
  - Every appearance cue prefers the wrong side.
  - For side-view animals the 2-D offset of a mirror pair is ≈ chance (near side vs far side).
  - Attempts that failed: handedness directions, a mirror-swap test, a TTA handedness term, a source-side semantic
    contrast, and NMS-peak pools.
- **About 45 other variants were within noise or negative:**
  - geometry: MNN-affine, MLS, dense flow, ICM-MRF, one-to-one assignment;
  - candidate pools: query smoothing, SD/DINO re-weighting, K, τ;
  - refinement: quadratic, up-sampled, Gaussian window;
  - TTA variants: grouped or separate source/target weights, low-rank adapters (collapse), other objectives, mixing,
    ensembles, arm re-selection;
  - T3 variants: joint attention, two-arm pool, peak pool, perm-drop.
- **Pitfall caught.** Bigger T3 models looked +1.2 better on dev but were memorising dev images, which are shared with
  T3's training data. An image-disjoint A/B protocol showed no gain, so T3 selection now uses that protocol.
- **Protocol notes.**
  - One run (seq 18) hit the 10-minute timeout. It was made faster and re-run; it was then discarded on merit.
  - The TTA no-mask test was re-run after an edit failed to apply.
