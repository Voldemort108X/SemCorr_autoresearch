# Daily report — Day 3 (2026-11-20 04:00 → 2026-11-21 04:00 UTC)

Today's work followed the three new tasks: (1) SOCO, (2) supervised features, (3) the paper's limitations.

**Units.**
- SOCO: SOC / CC = PCK@.10 against the geometrically correct instance (SOC) or any instance (CC). Computed per pair,
  then per class, then averaged over classes (OmniProbe protocol). Δ is relative to argmax.
- SPair: Δ = mean over PCK@.10/.05/.01 of the gain over top-1.
- **Selection.** SOCO decoders were selected on SOCO-dev (`trainsplits/train`, 10 pairs per class). Intra, cross and
  `trainsplits/test` are reports.

## 3. The paper's limitations
- **(a) Gap to top-k.** Of the keypoints whose top-1 is wrong but which have a correct candidate in the top 30 (SPair
  test @.10):

  | decoder | recoverable keypoints promoted | top-1 → top-30-oracle gap closed |
  |---|---|---|
  | Déjà Vu | 16.9 % | 8.1 % |
  | DV++ | 36.5 % | 26.5 % |
  | T3 | 44.5 % | 35.5 % |

  The pair-level (global) parts do the work.
- **(b) Mirror-equivalent parts.** On SOCO, the pair-level flip arm removes about half of the wrong-instance errors (see
  §1). On SPair, per-keypoint mirror swaps are unchanged (≈ 6 % for every decoder).
- **(c) Bird / horse.**

  | SPair test @.10 | top-1 | DV++ |
  |---|---|---|
  | horse | 73.8 | 76.4 |
  | bird | 89.6 | 89.4 |

  Bird is the only category below top-1; T3 gives bird +1.5.
